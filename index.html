Everything for your semester.

The full feature list, grouped by what you're trying to do.

AcademicsYour classes, teachers and grades.

  • Your Class Schedule

    Pick any semester. Raw SRTS codes become readable days and times (MW becomes Monday, Wednesday).

    • Shows course, section, room, instructor, credits and withdraw/drop dates.
    • Export to PDF: a clean, print-friendly landscape PDF of your routine.

  • Class reminders

    Notifications 30 and 10 minutes before every class, with course, section, time and room.

  • My Faculty Info

    Your instructors' names and hidden email addresses for any semester, with tap-to-copy.

  • All Faculty Info

    The full semester course, section, instructor and time list with live search by course, faculty or time.

  • CGPA Calculator

    Work out your semester GPA or your cumulative CGPA.

Tools and communitySmall things that save you an hour.

  • Scholarship Checker

    Enter your CGPA and admission term to see whether you qualify for 100% Merit, Dean's List or Medha Lalon, based on EWU's CGPA bands.

  • Assignment Cover Generator

    Creates the official EWU cover page as a PDF. Name and Student ID are pre-filled from your login, and the date defaults to today. Everything stays editable.

  • Exam Mode

    A focus lock that blocks distracting apps (social media, games and similar) while you study or sit an exam. It uses an Android Accessibility Service that you enable yourself.

  • Student Q&A Forum

    Ask questions and answer classmates.
