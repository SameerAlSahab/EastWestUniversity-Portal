EWU Portal App

A faster, cleaner, and more convenient way to access the East West University Student Portal.

Features

  • Secure Auto Login
    Save your portal credentials once and sign in without typing your ID and password every time.
  • Biometric Protection
    Unlock the app instantly using your fingerprint or device passcode while keeping your account secure.
  • Hidden Faculty Directory
    View faculty names and email addresses that are not easily visible inside the official portal.
  • Readable Class Schedule
    Schedules are converted from SRTS codes into normal weekday names with a clean mobile-friendly layout.
  • Class Schedule PDF
    Generate a well-formatted PDF version of your class schedule for printing or offline use.
  • Course & Result Viewer
    Quickly access your registered courses, semester results, CGPA, and other academic information.
  • File Upload Support
    Upload assignments and required documents directly from the app without opening a browser.
  • Mobile-First Interface
    Designed specifically for phones, with no horizontal scrolling or zooming required.
  • Faster Than the Browser
    Complete common portal tasks in fewer steps with a smoother experience than using the website.
  • Built for Everyday Use
    Everything is organized to reduce taps and save time during your daily university workflow.
